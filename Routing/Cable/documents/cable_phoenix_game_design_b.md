# Cable & Phoenix: Publisher Pitch Deck Draft

## High-Concept Summary
A roguelike timeline-loop game where the player alternates between **Cable** and a **Phoenix Host**. Cable fights to stabilize reality under constant internal decay, while the Phoenix wields overwhelming cosmic power at the cost of unraveling herself. Each run alters the future. Each failure teaches. Each success brings the universe closer to rebirth.

**Tagline:**
*Power that costs. Futures that loop. Salvation that must be earned.*

---

## Genre & Audience
- **Genre:** Roguelike Action Strategy, Dual-Protagonist Structure
- **Audience:** Players of *Hades*, *Returnal*, *Dead Cells*, *Slay the Spire*, *XCOM*
- **Appeal:** High replay value, emotional stakes, strong character identity, skill mastery.

---

## Gameplay Identity
The game is defined by **contrast**:
- **Cable Gameplay:** Tactical, constrained, strategic use of limited resources.
- **Phoenix Gameplay:** Powerful, expressive, destructive, but in constant danger of collapse.

Each run shifts between these modes, reinforcing the emotional and mechanical theme:
> **Strength always has a cost. Control is survival.**

---

## Core Gameplay Loop
1. **Start Timeline Run**
2. Play as **Cable** → stabilize environment → ration power vs. viral pressure.
3. Transition to **Phoenix Host** → reshape reality → manage stability decay.
4. Reach **Timeline Resolution Chamber** → choose how the universe evolves.
5. Earn **Hope Signals** → unlock permanent upgrades.
6. Begin a new run in a changed timeline.

---

## Progression System: Hope Signals
- Runs never truly reset.
- Every outcome feeds a **shared skill tree** based on the Summers-Grey lineage.
- The player slowly builds toward the **final stable future**.

Persistence mirrors narrative inevitability.

---

## Emotional Hook
This is a story about:
- **Holding yourself together when you could collapse.**
- **Choosing responsibility over impulse.**
- **Saving a future you may never get to see.**

The player *feels* Cable’s struggle:
- Just enough power to win… but always at risk of losing yourself.

The player *feels* the Phoenix’s burden:
- You can do anything… but doing too much destroys everything.

---

## Visual & Creative Direction (Style #3)
- Clean interfaces
- Structured information flow
- Transparent systems
- Readable state and resource indicators

Everything reinforces the game’s message:
> **Clarity is survival.**

## Final Statement
This game turns the Cable–Phoenix mythos into a modern interactive experience:
- **Skillful. Emotional. Replayable.**
- Every run changes the universe.
- Every choice shapes the future.

**The player isn’t just playing the story.**
**They are building the destiny of mutantkind, one timeline at a time.**
